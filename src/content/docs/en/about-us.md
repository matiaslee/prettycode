The content of this website is part of the Undergraduate Thesis for the Licentiate's Degree
in Computer Science at FaMAF (5 years program) by Francisco Zavalla,
titled: [Lineamientos para escribir código bonito](https://www.google.com/search?q=/src/assets/tesis_zavalla.pdf).
It was supervised by Dr. Matias David Lee and was defended in the year 2025, obtaining the maximum score.

When we started this project, one of the goals we had was for it to have impact.
This implies that the things we write get read. So, inspired by the concept of
[Continuous Delivery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_delivery),
we published the sections as soon as they were ready.
In the final chapter of the thesis, you can find the impact of this page from the publication
of the first section until the thesis defense.
